Standalone page. The app itself is untouched.

1 · Base layers

Zoom past the source's native max zoom and check that tiles upscale instead of vanishing.

2 · Custom layer with gaps

fallback: not active
Esri Wayback release 10 (2014) runs out of tiles around z16–18. Zoom in with fallback off to see the holes, then on.

3 · Points, rays, intersection

0 lines
Click twice to place a line. Drag a dot to move it (#12). Two or more lines give an intersection.

4 · Rotation

last right-click: –
Right-drag or Ctrl+drag rotates and tilts. A plain right-click should open the menu; a right-drag should not (#15).
Rotation uses MapLibre's linear mode (aroundCenter: false). Add ?aroundCenter to the URL to feel the default hybrid mode again.

5 · Terrain

Free Terrarium elevation tiles (AWS, no key). Place a point on a slope with terrain on, then switch terrain off: the dot should stay on the same imagery feature.

6 · Zoom offset

The URL hash is MapLibre's built-in one (zoom/lat/lng/bearing/pitch), relevant for #14.
