How TracePoint works
  1. Load a photo: drop it, paste it, or browse
  2. Level it (optional): fix a tilted horizon with the level button
  3. Add a line: drag it onto a road, shadow or building edge
  4. Place 2 points: click the line twice, then both spots on the map
  5. Fine-tune: drag a point, or its marker on the map
  6. Repeat: two rays cross at the camera; a third adds an ellipse
